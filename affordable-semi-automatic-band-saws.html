BS-315GF full-hydraulic double-miter band saw, 10″

BS-315GF

Our most popular value-line saw: a 10-inch gear-driven semi-automatic with everything hydraulic — the vise clamps hydraulically, the bow descends on a hydraulic cylinder at a dialled-in feed rate and lifts itself hydraulically at the end of the cut. The bow swivels to both sides for 30°, 45° and 60° miters with fast angle stops, the gear-driven motor runs the band at a variable blade speed set on the VFD inverter drive. One operator loads, clamps and starts; the saw does the rest.

  • Ø10″ (250 mm) round / 12-1/2″ × 7-1/2″ at 0°; Ø9″ at 45° both sides, Ø5-7/8″ at 60°
  • Hydraulic clamping vise, hydraulic down-feed with variable feed rate and hydraulic bow up-lift
  • Swivels left and right with fast angle stops at 30°, 45° and 60°
  • Gear-driven 1.5 HP (1.1 kW) blade motor with variable blade speed by VFD inverter drive, up to 262 ft/min (80 m/min), and a separate 1 HP (0.75 kW) motor for the hydraulic pump
  • Bow pivots on adjustable tapered bearings — no backlash; electro-mechanical blade tensioner with microswitch
  • Safety guards on the flywheel casing and blade; electric coolant system; 1″ × .035″ × 111″ (27 × 0.9 × 2825 mm) blade

Questions we get asked about NovoTech semi-automatic band saws

What is the difference between a semi-automatic and a fully automatic band saw?

A semi-automatic saw makes one cut per start: it clamps, cuts and lifts the bow, then waits for the operator to feed the next piece. A fully automatic saw also feeds the material to length and repeats the cycle for the programmed quantity — see the value line automatic band saws.

Which value-line saws cut miters on both sides?

The BS-315GF, BS-460G / 460G-JC and BS-650GA swivel to both sides (45° left and right, 60° right); the BS-2114TH cuts angles up to 60° on one side.

What does "full-hydraulic" mean on the BS-315GF?

Hydraulic vise, hydraulic down-feed and hydraulic bow up-lift on one 10-inch machine: the operator loads the bar, presses start and the saw clamps, cuts, lifts the bow and switches off. The gear drive and a bow that swivels to both sides with fast stops at 30°, 45° and 60° complete it.

What does SC mean?

Single column: the BS-5030 guides the bow vertically on a column instead of a pivot, for square straight cuts up to Ø11-3/4″ / 19-3/4″ × 11-3/4″ with a PLC touch screen. Its automatic sister is the BS-5040.

What voltage do these saws run on?

Three-phase machines are supplied for 600 V with a CSA-approved electrical panel; 208 V, 240 V or 480 V 3-phase are available on request.
