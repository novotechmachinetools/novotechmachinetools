Welding positioners — tilting, rotary and lift, 22 to 44,092 lb

A welding positioner holds the part on a turntable and turns it under the torch at a steady, adjustable speed, so a round weld on a flange, pipe-to-plate joint, hub, tank head or wheel is laid in one continuous pass in the flat position — no stopping, repositioning and restarting around the part, and a more even bead whether the torch is held by hand or clamped in a holder. Tilting the table brings the joint to the best angle for the torch. NovoTech has positioners for every size of part, all on this page, small to large: light and benchtop positioners from 22 to 247 lb, with chucks, hollow bores and foot pedals; the premium-line WP 100 and WP 300 tilting positioners, built in Turkey, 220 and 661 lb on single-phase power; value-line tilting positioners from 485 to 2,646 lb; HBT rotary welding tables and HBJ lift positioners that also adjust in height, to 44,092 lb; heavy tilting positioners from 3,307 to 44,092 lb with motorized tilt; headstock and tailstock, L-type and hydraulic positioners built to order; and the chucks and torch holders that go on them. Each family below has its full specification table and photos. Parts, installation and service from Mississauga, ON, Canada.

  • Light and benchtop positioners 22 – 247 lb · premium-line WP 100 and WP 300 tilting positioners, 220 and 661 lb, single-phase
  • Tilting positioners to 2,646 lb · heavy HB and WP positioners to 44,092 lb with motorized tilt
  • HBT rotary welding tables and HBJ lift positioners to 44,092 lb · headstock and tailstock positioners built to order

Questions we get asked about welding positioners

What does a welding positioner do?

It turns the part under a stationary torch at a set speed, so a circumferential weld is made in one pass in the flat position — the easiest position to weld and the one that gives the most even bead. The welder no longer walks around the part or stops to reposition it; with the torch in a holder the travel speed is as steady as the table, which makes repeat parts faster and more uniform.

How do I size a positioner for my part?

By the weight and where its centre of gravity sits. The rating is for the centre of gravity close to the table centre and face: the further it is from the axis (an off-centre bracket) or from the table (a tall part), the more torque the drive and the tilt need. The tables give the limits — the HB positioners up to 3.9 – 9.8″ off the axis and 5.9 – 23.6″ above the table, the heavy WP positioners 12″ and 12″. For off-centre or tall parts choose the next size up, and send us a sketch with the weight.

How fast should the table turn?

Table speed (rpm) = travel speed ÷ (π × weld diameter). A MIG weld at 20 in/min around a Ø6″ flange needs about 1.1 rpm; a submerged-arc weld at 20 in/min on a Ø60″ tank head about 0.1 rpm. Check that the positioner's speed range covers your smallest and largest weld diameters: the light positioners turn up to 8 – 18 rpm, the heavy ones as slowly as 0.03 rpm.

Tilting positioner, rotary table, lift or headstock positioner?

A tilting positioner turns and tilts the part on a table — the most versatile. A rotary welding table (HBT) only turns, for parts that stand upright: tanks, drums, rings and hubs. A lift positioner (HBJ) also adjusts the table height, so the work comes to the welder or the welding head. Headstock and tailstock positioners turn long parts — beams, columns, frames and shafts — about their own length.

What can be mounted on the turntable?

The tables are T-slotted for clamps, angle plates and fixtures. A self-centring 3-jaw welding chuck holds pipe, shafts and round parts on centre — the WPC chucks on this page grip from Ø0.4″ inside to Ø15″ outside — and a torch holder on an adjustable arm keeps the torch in one place while the part turns.

Premium-line or value-line?

The WP 100 and WP 300 tilting positioners are built in Turkey for NovoTech's premium line and run on single-phase 220 V with a foot pedal. The other positioners, the turning rolls, the column and boom manipulators, the pipe stands and the tables are NovoTech's value line, built in China to NovoTech's specification — with the same CSA-approved panel, 1-year warranty and parts and support in Canada.

What voltage do these machines run on?

Three-phase machines are supplied for 600 V as standard with a CSA-approved panel; 208 V, 240 V and 480 V 3-phase are available on request, and 240 V single-phase on models whose main motor is 2.2 kW (3 HP) or less — a machine with a motor above 2.2 kW must run on three-phase power and cannot be converted to 240 V single-phase. Manual machines have no electrics.
