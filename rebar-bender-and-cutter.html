Questions we get asked about rebar benders and cutters

Which rebar bender do I need for 15M, 20M and 25M bars?

A B 36 or BS 36 bends single bars up to 35M (#11) and two 20M bars at once in normal steel, and is the usual choice for a rebar shop running 10M to 25M. For site work on 10M–20M a portable BT 26 or MG 26 Junior on 110/220 V is enough. For 30M–45M bars choose a B 45x1, B 50 or the BS 50/BS 60.

Can I run a rebar bender on single-phase power?

Yes — the portable MG 20B, MG 26 Junior, BT 24x5 and BT 26 benders and the POWER 24 cutter have a built-in transformer and run on 110 V or 220 V single-phase. The stationary B, BS, SL, SX, H, M and SH machines are three-phase and are supplied for 600 V; 208 V, 240 V or 480 V are available on request.

Do the machines come with a CSA-approved electrical panel?

Yes. Every machine NovoTech supplies has a CSA-approved electrical panel wired for Canadian voltage, with overload protection on the benders.

Can a standard rebar bender make stirrups?

Yes. The B series and the portable BT benders make stirrups with the standard pins and rollers; an optional spiral stirrup attachment is available for B and BS series only. For continuous stirrup production from coil use the automatic SLS 12 on the automatic lines page.

What does the 45 / 65 / 85 kg/mm² capacity mean?

The three columns are the manufacturer's ratings for rebar of different tensile strengths: 45 kg/mm² (≈ 64,000 psi), 65 kg/mm² (≈ 92,500 psi) and 85 kg/mm² (≈ 120,900 psi). Canadian Grade 400W rebar has a minimum tensile strength of 540 MPa (≈ 55 kg/mm²), so the 65 kg/mm² column is a safe guide for everyday Canadian bar.

Are spare parts, blades and tooling available in Canada?

Yes. NovoTech stocks cutting blades, bending pins, rollers and electrical parts for GMS machines in Mississauga, ON, and provides technical support, installation and training.
