Bekamak double-column band saws

For heavy bar, billets, beams and bundles the bow runs between two hardened columns instead of pivoting, so the blade enters the material squarely and stays square through the cut. Bekamak builds semi-automatic and fully automatic versions, miter heads to 60°, servo feeding with NC job lists and the enclosed high-tech XS production saws — from Ø13″ to 59″ (330–1500 mm). Known as double column, twin-column or dual column metal bandsaws, these horizontal band saw machines are the industrial choice for heavy solids, bundles and plate.

  • Twin-column guidance for square cuts in heavy stock
  • Semi-automatic, roller-feed and servo-feed NC models
  • Round capacities from Ø13″ (330 mm) to 59″ (1500 mm)

BMSY 330C · BMSY 360C · BMSY 460C · BMSY 560C · BMSY 810C · BMSY 825C · BMSY 1020C

Questions we get asked about Bekamak double-column band saws

Why choose a double-column saw over a pivot saw?

Rigidity and capacity. The bow is guided on two columns, so there is no side load on the blade — cuts stay square in large solids and the blade lasts longer. Double-column saws also reach capacities a pivot bow cannot: 330 mm up to 1500 mm on the Bekamak range.

Can a double-column saw cut miters?

Yes — the BMSY CGH (single miter) and BMSY DG NC (double miter) swivel the whole bow assembly up to 60° under hydraulic or NC control, and the automatic CGS/CDGS NC models do the same with servo feeding.

What is a split vise (SV)?

The SV versions of the BMSY C saws have a second vise jaw close to the blade, so short pieces and remnants are clamped on both sides of the cut — useful for cutting slices from blocks and billets.

What is the XS line?

Bekamak's high-tech production saws: servo-driven bow feed and material feed, full enclosure and CNC control, designed for carbide blades, the fastest cycle times and unattended running.
