Trumpf and Wila-style (New Standard) press brake tooling — punches and dies

EUROSTAMP tooling for press brakes with Trumpf and Wila (New Standard) clamping — Trumpf, Darley, Safan, RPC and the other makers that use this hydraulic clamping. Trumpf-style punches stand 140 to 300 mm (5.51 – 11.81″) tall for deep boxes, with dies from V6 to V120 in 100, 120 and 150 mm heights; Wila-style punches run from 135 to 237 mm and the dies are 55 or 100 mm high. Tools up to 13.5 kg (Trumpf style) and 12.5 kg (Wila style) come with safety buttons that stop a released tool from falling, so they load from the front. 42CrMo4 and C45 steel, induction-hardened, with a DXF profile of every tool.

  • Trumpf-style punches 5.51 – 11.81″ (140 – 300 mm) high, rated to 84 US t/ft (250 t/m); dies V .24 – 4.72″
  • Wila-style punches 5.31 – 9.33″ (135 – 237 mm); dies 55 and 100 mm (2.17 / 3.94″) high
  • Safety buttons standard on the lighter tools; 500 / 515 mm lengths and 550 mm sectioned sets

Questions we get asked about Trumpf and Wila-style tooling

Are Trumpf-style and Wila-style tools interchangeable?

They share the New Standard clamping idea — a vertical tang with safety buttons that the hydraulic clamp pulls up and seats — and EUROSTAMP lists Trumpf, Darley and Safan brakes for both. Check the tang and the heights of the tools you already run before mixing them, and send us the clamp model if you are unsure; we confirm before you order.

What do the safety buttons do?

The spring-loaded buttons in the tang catch the tool in the clamp while it is released, so it can be loaded and removed from the front without falling. EUROSTAMP fits them as standard on Trumpf-style tools up to 13.5 kg (30 lb) and Wila-style tools up to 12.5 kg (27.5 lb); heavier tools are loaded from the side.

Which height should I order?

Match the height of the tools already in the machine so the punch tips and die shoulders stay on the same line — mixing heights means re-setting the ram. Use the tall 220 – 300 mm punches only where the part needs the depth, as the load rating falls with height.

Can we get the tools as DXF files for our CNC?

Yes — every tool in these tables that has a DXF link can be downloaded straight from the page: the profile drawn in millimetres, ready to import into the tool library of a Delem, ESA or Cybelec control or into offline bending software. For the few tools without one, ask us and we get the drawing from EUROSTAMP.
