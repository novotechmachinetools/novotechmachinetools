Bekamak semi-automatic band saws

The workhorse of most fabrication shops: the operator loads the bar and presses start; the hydraulic vise clamps, the bow cuts at the set feed rate and lifts clear when the cut is done. Bekamak's BMSY pivot saws come with single- or double-miter heads for structural steel, inverter blade speed and hydro-mechanical blade tension — from Ø9″ to Ø17-3/8″ (230–440 mm). If you are looking for a semi-automatic metal bandsaw, a hydraulic horizontal band saw for metal or an industrial band saw machine with a hydraulic vise, this is the page.

  • Single- or double-miter heads to 60°; DGH models add a hydraulic vise
  • Round capacities from Ø9″ (230 mm) to Ø17-3/8″ (440 mm)

Questions we get asked about Bekamak semi-automatic band saws

What is the difference between a semi-automatic and a fully automatic band saw?

A semi-automatic saw makes one cut per start: it clamps, cuts and lifts the bow, then waits for the operator to feed the next piece. A fully automatic saw also feeds the material to length (by rollers or a shuttle vise) and repeats the cycle for the programmed quantity. See the Bekamak automatic band saws page.

Single miter or double miter?

A single-miter head (BMSY 320G/GL) swivels to one side, so angled parts are cut by flipping the bar. A double-miter head (BMSY DG/DGH) swivels both ways — +60° right, −45° left — which saves handling on frames and trusses with opposite angles.

What does the DGH suffix mean?

DGH models have a hydraulic vise — the DG versions clamp the material by hand. The rest of the saw is the same: double-miter head swung by hand on the angle scale, hydraulic feed cylinder and automatic bow return.

Can these saws cut bundles?

Yes, with the optional hydraulic top clamping, which presses a bundle of tubes or bars down while the side vise holds it. Ask us about bundle sizes for the model you need.
