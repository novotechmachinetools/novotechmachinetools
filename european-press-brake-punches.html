European-style (Amada-Promecam) press brake punches — straight, acute, gooseneck, same-height, radius and offset tools

EUROSTAMP punches for press brakes with European-style clamping — the Amada-Promecam standard used by Amada and Promecam and by dozens of other makers, from Accurpress, Baykal and Dener to Durmazlar, Ermaksan, Prima Power, Salvagnini and Yawei. Made in Italy from C45 and 42CrMo4 steel, induction-hardened to 55 – 60 HRC on the working surfaces, in 835 mm (32.9″) and 415 mm (16.3″) lengths and 805 mm (31.7″) sectioned sets with horns. Six families on this page: straight punches from 45° to 88° and heavy-duty punches with R6 – R10 tips for plate; acute 24° to 35° punches; gooseneck and sash punches for return flanges; the ESH series with one 120 mm working height for staged bending; radius holders with round elements R3 to R50; and offset (joggle) tools. The punches link to their DXF profiles for your CNC, and NovoTech quotes, ships and supports the tooling from Mississauga, ON, Canada.

  • Straight 45° – 88°, acute 24° – 35°, gooseneck and sash punches 2.56 – 7.87″ (65 – 200 mm) high, rated to 50 US t/ft (150 t/m)
  • ESH same-height series: every punch 120 mm (4.72″), for staged bending in one setup
  • Radius holders with round elements R3 – R50 and offset tools Z 1 – 15 mm; DXF profiles to download

Straight and heavy-duty punches, 45° – 88°

1011 – 1339 · 24 profiles

EUROSTAMP press brake tooling — punches and dies

The everyday punch for air bending to 90°: a straight body with an 88° or 85° tip — 85° leaves room to over-bend stainless and high-strength steel that springs back further — in working heights from 65 to 135 mm (2.56 – 5.31″), plus 75°, 60° and 45° punches for acute bends and deeper parts. Tip radii run from 0.25 mm for crisp corners in thin sheet to 3 mm, and the four heavy-duty punches carry R6 to R10 tips and up to 150 t/m (50 US t/ft) for plate, where a sharp tip would crack the outside of the bend.

  • Angles 88°, 85°, 75°, 60° and 45°; working heights 65 to 200 mm (2.56 – 7.87″)
  • Tip radius R0.25 to R3 for sheet; heavy-duty profiles with R6 to R10 tips, rated to 150 t/m (50 US t/ft)
  • 835 mm (32.9″) and 415 mm (16.3″) full lengths; 805 mm (31.7″) sectioned set — 100 mm horns left and right plus 10, 15, 20, 40, 50, 200 and 270 mm pieces
  • Moving horns for 1011, 1047 and 1065 (codes 1011S, 1047S, 1065S, pairs of 150 mm) to bend flanges at the end of a part
  • C45 or 42CrMo4 steel, induction-hardened 55 – 60 HRC up to 3 mm (.118″) deep
  • DXF profiles for the CNC's tool library

Acute and hemming punches, 24° – 35°

1033 – 1311 · 20 profiles

Acute punches bend well past 90° — to 30° or 35° in a matching die — for the first stage of a hem, for acute flanges and to over-bend high-strength steel. The 26°, 30° and 35° punches come in working heights from 80 to 200 mm (3.15 – 7.87″) with tip radii from 0.5 to 5 mm; the slim-body 30° punch reaches into narrow profiles. The 24° and 28° hemming punches pre-bend the flange that a hemming die then closes flat in the second stroke.

  • Angles 35°, 30° and 26°; hemming punches 28° and 24°
  • Working heights 80 to 200 mm (3.15 – 7.87″); tip radius R0.5 to R5
  • Rated to 100 t/m (34 US t/ft) — check the rating of slim and tall profiles before bending plate
  • 835 and 415 mm (32.9 / 16.3″) lengths and the 805 mm (31.7″) sectioned set with horns
  • Pairs with the 30° and 45° dies and the hemming dies on the European dies page

Gooseneck and sash punches, 60° – 88°

1014 – 1322 · 32 profiles

A gooseneck punch is relieved behind the tip so a returned flange clears the body: the punch for U-channels, pans and boxes with return flanges. Thirty-odd profiles from 66.6 to 200 mm (2.62 – 7.87″) high — single, double and reversed goosenecks, 60° and 85° goosenecks for acute returns and springy materials, and sash punches with the slim nose for tight return flanges and window profiles. The tall 145, 165 and 200 mm punches reach into deep boxes; check the load rating, which falls as the neck gets deeper.

  • Single, double and reversed goosenecks, 88°, 85° and 60°; sash punches 88°
  • Working heights 66.6 to 200 mm (2.62 – 7.87″); tip radius R0.25 to R3
  • Rated to 100 t/m (34 US t/ft); deep-necked profiles lower — see the table
  • 835 and 415 mm (32.9 / 16.3″) lengths and the 805 mm (31.7″) sectioned set with horns; the 200 mm profiles in 505 mm and 805 mm sectioned
  • C45 or 42CrMo4 steel, induction-hardened 55 – 60 HRC

Choosing press brake tooling

EUROSTAMP press brake tooling — introduction

Questions we get asked about European-style punches

Will these punches fit my press brake?

If your press brake clamps European-style (Amada-Promecam) tooling, yes. EUROSTAMP lists Amada, Promecam, Accurpress, Adira, Baykal, Boschert, Bystronic-Beyeler Euro-B, Colgar, Dener, Durmazlar, Ermaksan, Gasparini, Haco, Prima Power, Salvagnini, Yawei and many more as using this style. If you are not sure, send us a photo of the upper clamp and a punch you use now — or the model of the brake — and we confirm before you order. Adapters let other press brakes take these punches.

Straight or gooseneck punch?

A straight punch is stiffer and carries more load, so it is the first choice for open angles and plate. A gooseneck is relieved behind the tip: use it when a flange you have already bent comes back towards the punch — U-channels, pans, boxes with return flanges. The deeper the neck, the lower the load rating, so check the table before you bend thick plate on a gooseneck.

What tip radius should I choose?

In air bending the inside radius is set mostly by the V opening — about 1/6 of the V in mild steel — not by the punch. Choose a punch radius at or below that natural radius: R0.6 to R0.8 for sheet up to about 10 gauge, R1.5 to R3 for 3/16 to 1/4″, and the heavy-duty R6 – R10 punches for plate thicker than about 3/8″, where a sharp tip would crack the outside of the bend. A sharp R0.25 punch gives crisp corners in thin stainless and aluminum.

What are horns and sectioned sets?

A sectioned set is a punch or die cut into pieces that add up to a standard length — for European-style punches 805 mm (31.7″) made of 100 mm horns at each end and 10, 15, 20, 40, 50, 200 and 270 mm pieces. You assemble the exact length of a bend, and the horns — the ends relieved on the outside — let you bend the sides of a box without hitting the flanges already formed.

What is the ESH series?

ESH stands for EUROSTAMP Same Height: every punch in the series — straight, gooseneck, sash, 86°, 60°, 30° and 26° — has the same 120 mm working height, and the matching insert dies the same 60 mm. Several tools can be set up side by side in stations and used without re-setting the ram, which saves set-up time on parts with several different bends.

Can we get the tools as DXF files for our CNC?

Yes — every tool in these tables that has a DXF link can be downloaded straight from the page: the profile drawn in millimetres, ready to import into the tool library of a Delem, ESA or Cybelec control or into offline bending software. For the few tools without one, ask us and we get the drawing from EUROSTAMP.
