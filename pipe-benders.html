Pipe benders and tube bending machines

A pipe bender or tube bending machine draws a tube around a bend die to a set angle; a mandrel bender adds an internal mandrel so thin-wall tube bends to a tight radius without wrinkling or collapsing. Every İşitan tube and pipe bender NovoTech sells in Canada: servo CNC mandrel benders with 3D simulation for production, NC hydraulic mandrel benders with a 10″ touchscreen for exhaust, handrail and furniture shops, compact SFB 32 and aluminum-profile benders, and BK rotary draw benders for pipe, bar and box section — from a 1 HP bench bender to a 34 HP CNC pipe bending machine for Ø3-1/2″ × 8 ga. Built in Turkey by İşitan since 1959, supplied with CSA-approved panels, one tooling set and installation, and supported from NovoTech's warehouse in Mississauga, ON. Questions we get asked about pipe benders

Pipe bender, tube bender, mandrel bender — what is the difference?

"Pipe" is sized by nominal pipe size (NPS) and schedule, "tube" by outside diameter and wall, but the machines are the same: a bend die, a clamp and a pressure die draw the material around the radius. A mandrel bender adds an internal mandrel so thin-wall tube can be bent to a tight radius without wrinkling; our SFB benders are mandrel benders, the BK series are rotary draw benders without a mandrel. Every capacity on these pages is given as OD × wall in inches and gauge.

How do I choose a tube bender?

Start with the largest tube (OD × wall and material), the tightest centreline radius and the number of bends per part — that sets mandrel or not and the model size. Then the volume: a rotary draw BK for occasional bends, an SFB NC for programmed angles with manual feed, the NC/C carrier for repeatable multi-plane parts, and a CNC when parts come from a drawing in hundreds. Send us a part drawing and we will size the machine and tooling.

Which tube benders does NovoTech stock?

We keep the SFB 38-NC and 51-NC and the BK-050 in Mississauga, ON, and bring CNC models, carrier models and the larger 76 and 90 sizes in to order with their tooling. In-stock machines carry the 7-day money-back guarantee.

Do you install tube benders and train operators?

Yes — our technicians commission the bender anywhere in Canada, set up the first tooling, prove out your part and train operators on the NC or CNC. Remote assistance over the network is built into the CNC models, and we support every machine by video call.

What do you need from me to quote tooling?

Six things: material type, tube or profile size, wall thickness, the smallest centreline radius (CLR) you bend, the maximum bend angle and the maximum tube length. Every bender comes with one die set in the size you request; extra sets, ball mandrels and wiper dies are quoted from the same list.

What voltage do the benders run on?

Three-phase machines are supplied for 600 V as standard with a CSA-approved panel; 208 V, 240 V and 480 V 3-phase are available on request, and 240 V single-phase on the BK-42 and other models with a small enough motor.
