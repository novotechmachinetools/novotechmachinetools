Circle cutting machines and lock seamers

Circle cutting machines (circle shears, circle cutters) cut round blanks and rings from sheet for tank heads, fan housings, flanges, lids and gaskets, and with the flanging attachment turn the edge of the disc for dished heads. İşitan's IDK series runs from the hand-operated IDK-1 to the hydraulic IDK-5 cutting 78.7″ discs in 3/16″ steel; the LS-13P lock seaming machine closes the longitudinal seam of round ducts. Sold with CSA-approved panels and supported from Mississauga, ON.

  • IDK-5: discs to Ø78.7″ (2000 mm) in 3/16″ steel, hydraulic clamp, flanging option
  • Motorized IDK-3.5 / 2 / 1.8 and manual IDK-1 for 10–20 ga
  • LS-13P pneumatic lock seamer — 51″ seams in 18 ga

Circle cutters and lock seamer line-up

IDK circle cutting machines — hydraulic, motorized and manual circle shears

IDK-5 · IDK-3.5 · IDK-2 · IDK-1.8 · IDK-1

Circle cutting shears cut round blanks — and on the IDK-5, 3.5 and 2 also inside diameters (rings) — from sheet clamped at its centre and rotated between two circular blades. The IDK-5 is the production machine: hydraulic centre clamp, motorized cutting-diameter adjustment, discs up to 78.7″ in 3/16″ steel and an optional flanging attachment that turns the edge of the disc up to 3/8″ for tank heads. The IDK-3.5 adds a pneumatic clamp to a 47″ machine, the IDK-2 and IDK-1.8 are rigid cast-frame motorized cutters for 14 ga, and the IDK-1 is the hand-operated bench model.

  • IDK-5: welded steel frame, hydraulic centre clamp, motorized cutting-diameter adjustment, inside-diameter cutting, mobile control panel
  • IDK-3.5: pneumatic centre clamp (116 psi air supply), motorized diameter adjustment, inside-diameter cutting
  • IDK-2: rigid cast frame, blades in bearings, foot pedal, inside-diameter cutting
  • IDK-1.8: rigid cast frame, blades in bronze bushings, foot pedal
  • Special-steel (1.2379) blades; shafts on conical bearings for long blade life; gearbox motor with brake
  • Built to EC safety directives (CE)

LS-13P pneumatic heavy-duty lock seaming machine

LS-13P

A motorized lock seamer that closes the longitudinal lock seam of round ducts and pipes: a gearbox-driven pinching system with pneumatic pressing, hardened seaming rolls on a ball-bearing slide, and pinching and rotation controlled from the foot pedal. Seams up to 51″ long in 18 ga sheet on pipes of 3.9″ to 5.1″ diameter — the companion to the slip roll and the bead roller in the HVAC shop.

  • Gearbox-driven pinching system; pneumatic pressing for easy operation
  • Pinching and rotation controlled by the standard foot pedal
  • Hardened seaming rolls; sliding system on ball bearings
  • Pneumatic system protected by air filter
  • Operator's manual

Questions we get asked about circle cutting machines and lock seamers

How does a circle cutting machine work?

The sheet is clamped at its centre on a spindle and turned by hand or motor while two rotary blades shear a circle at the radius set on the arm. On the IDK-5, 3.5 and 2 a second pass at a smaller radius cuts the inside diameter, leaving a ring; the blank can then be flanged on the IDK-5's optional attachment.

What size and thickness can be cut?

From Ø4.3″ up to Ø78.7″ (2 m) depending on the model, in mild steel from 20 ga on the manual IDK-1 to 3/16″ on the hydraulic IDK-5. Stainless steel reduces the capacity by roughly one gauge; ask us for the model that suits your material.

Hydraulic, pneumatic or manual clamp?

The centre clamp holds the sheet on the spindle while it rotates. The IDK-5 clamps hydraulically and the IDK-3.5 pneumatically (116 psi shop air) — fast and consistent for production; the IDK-2, 1.8 and 1 clamp by hand screw, which is fine for a few parts at a time.

What does the LS-13P lock seamer do?

After a sheet is rolled into a pipe on a slip roll with a Pittsburgh or snap lock profile, the LS-13P closes the longitudinal seam: the pneumatic rolls pinch the lock over the full 51″ length while the pipe is rotated with the foot pedal, giving a tight, straight seam on 3.9–5.1″ ducts in 18 ga.

What voltage do these machines run on?

Three-phase machines are supplied for 600 V as standard with a CSA-approved panel; 208 V, 240 V and 480 V 3-phase are available on request, and 240 V single-phase on models with a small enough motor. Manual machines have no electrics.
