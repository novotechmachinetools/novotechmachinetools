Window production lines — PVC and aluminum production groups for 40 to 220 windows a shift

A window shop is a flow of machines, and its output is set by the slowest of them. Plastmak plans production groups in which every step keeps pace with the others, and NovoTech supplies four of them: a PVC single-head group (six machines, 40 – 60 windows in an 8-hour shift), a PVC double-head group (six machines, 80 – 120 windows), an aluminum double-head group (four machines, 80 – 120 windows) and a complete PVC workshop laid out as a loop (seven machines, 200 – 220 windows). Each table lists the machines with their capacity, power, air and weight, and the totals for the group. Machines can be swapped or added to suit your profiles and output; we plan the layout, install the line and train your team from Mississauga, ON, Canada.

  • PVC single-head group: 6 machines, 40 – 60 windows a shift
  • PVC double-head and aluminum double-head groups: 80 – 120 windows a shift

Questions we get asked about window production lines

How many windows will a group make?

Plastmak's figures for an 8-hour shift are 40 – 60 windows for the PVC single-head group, 80 – 120 for the PVC and aluminum double-head groups and 200 – 220 for the complete workshop. The real number depends on window sizes and types, the profile system and how many people run the line — tell us your mix and we will estimate it.

Can I start small and grow?

Yes. The groups share machines: the copy router, end mill, bead saw and corner cleaner of the single-head group stay when a double-head saw and welder are added, and a CNC corner cleaner, a 3-axis CNC or a 4-corner welder can follow later.

What do I need on site?

Three-phase power for the installed load in each table, compressed air at 87 – 116 psi (6 – 8 bar) for the consumption listed — the workshop group includes a 130 gal (500 l) compressor — and floor space for the machines in a loop, with room for 21-ft profiles. We provide a layout before delivery.

What voltage do these machines run on?

Three-phase machines are supplied for 600 V as standard with a CSA-approved panel; 208 V, 240 V and 480 V 3-phase are available on request, and 240 V single-phase on models whose main motor is 2.2 kW (3 HP) or less — a machine with a motor above 2.2 kW must run on three-phase power and cannot be converted to 240 V single-phase. Manual machines have no electrics.
