Senfeng's Intelligent Air Cutting, co-developed with BOCHU Automation for the H4, NP and R series from 6 to 60 kW, lets the laser cut with clean, dry compressed air from its own bus-connected compressor instead of bottled nitrogen or oxygen — and keeps oxygen and nitrogen for the jobs that need them. Hardware, control system, cutting software and service are upgraded together: faster, cleaner cuts at a much lower running cost, and simpler operation.

Lower running cost
  • Assist gas close to free — no nitrogen or oxygen to buy for most work; Senfeng quotes overall cutting expenses down by up to 92 %

  • Clean air protects the optics — protective-lens use drops from 5 a day to 2

Faster, cleaner cuts
  • Carbon steel cuts much faster than with oxygen; thin-sheet throughput up by more than 30 %

Shuttle table or pallet changer — what is the difference?

Both load the next sheet while the first is cut. A shuttle table (GFL, Smart, ULE, compact) has two tables that swap places in 15 – 30 seconds, one in the cabin cutting and one outside being loaded. A pallet changer (Senfeng H series) lifts the pallet with the cut sheet out of the cabin and slides the loaded pallet in, which is the arrangement a loading tower and sheet storage connect to for lights-out production. For a few sheets an hour the shuttle table is enough; for automation, the pallet changer. Some makers call the shuttle table an exchange table, a double table or a dual table — it is the same thing.

Premium line or value line?

The premium GFL and Smart are built by Dener in Turkey on stress-relieved frames — the GFL rated for 1.5 G — with Boji BLT autofocus heads and Raycus sources (Precitec and IPG on request), longer life at high duty and parts stocked in Canada: the machines for two shifts a day. NovoTech's value-line ULE and the Senfeng H series, NP and compact lasers are built in China with their own or Raycus sources in a wider range — 4 × 4 ft machines, 60 – 80 kW — at a lower price; NovoTech stocks the parts and does the service. Same cut quality at the same power.

What laser power do I need?

Power sets the thickness and the speed. As a guide for mild steel: 3 kW cuts 1/2″, 6 kW 3/4″ (and 1/4″ at over 300 in/min), 12 kW 1″, 20 kW 1-1/4″ and 30 – 40 kW 1-1/2″ and beyond; stainless and aluminum run about 20 – 30 % thinner at the same power, with nitrogen or air assist. Most Canadian job shops buying today choose 6 or 12 kW: thick enough for structural work, fast enough to pay for the machine on thin sheet. Which table size?

The model number is the nominal working area in centimetres: 3015 = 10 × 5 ft (3,000 × 1,500 mm) — a standard 4 × 8 or 5 × 10 sheet; 4020 = 13 × 6.6 ft; 6020 = 20 × 6.6 ft for 6 × 20 plate; 6025 and 8025 = 20 and 26 ft × 8.2 ft; 12526 = 41 × 8.5 ft. The 5 × 10 machine is the Canadian standard; buy the 6020 if you cut 20 ft plate or want to nest several sheets without reloading; the 4 × 4 ft Senfeng compact is for small parts in a small shop.

Can the laser be automated?

Yes — a loading / unloading tower that feeds the pallet changer, sheet storage with several shelves for lights-out production, part sorting, and bevel heads for weld preparation. The Dener GFL and the Smart with shuttle table, the NovoTech ULE and the Senfeng H series are prepared for the tower; the Senfeng coil-fed lines go further and cut straight from the coil. What about assist gas and fume extraction?

Oxygen gives the fastest cut and an oxide edge on mild steel; nitrogen a clean, paint-ready edge on all metals; compressed air at 16 bar replaces nitrogen on sheet to about 1/4″ and cuts the gas bill to near zero — the intelligent air-cutting package is an option on both lines. The machines have pneumatic extraction compartments under the table and connect to a dust collector, which we can supply.
