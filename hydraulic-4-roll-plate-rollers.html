Hydraulic 4-roll plate rollers

Four-roll plate rolling machines pinch the sheet between the top and bottom roll and bend it with two side rolls, so the plate is pre-bent at both ends and rolled into a full cylinder in one pass without flipping it. İşitan's 4R HMS hydraulic plate rollers run from a 2.1 ft bench-size roll to 10.2 ft machines for 3/8″ plate, with NC control, overhead crane and sheet feeder for production work; the 4R OM and 4R HOM roll corrugated galvanized sheet for tanks, roofs and silos. Every 4 roll plate roller is sold with a CSA-approved panel and supported from Mississauga, ON.

4R HMS hydraulic 4-roll plate rollers

4R HMS 650x80 – 4R HMS 3100x250 · 36 models

İşitan's main line of 4-roll plate rolling machines, from a 2.1 ft bench-size roll to 10.2 ft machines that roll 3/8″ plate. The pinch roll and both side rolls move on a pyramid-style linear path driven by hydraulic cylinders, while the top roll is powered by an electric motor through a planetary gearbox — so the sheet is pinched, pre-bent at both ends and rolled into a full cylinder without being removed from the machine. Cone bending, hydraulic drop-end and three digital readouts are standard; NC control with programmed roll positions, overhead crane and side supports turn it into a semi- or fully automatic production line.

  • Pinch and side rolls moved linearly by hydraulic cylinders; top roll driven by motor and planetary gearbox, bottom roll through a cardan joint
  • Three digital readouts for easy roll positioning; mobile control panel
  • Hydraulic drop-end for easy removal of finished cylinders; hydraulic balancing system
  • Induction-hardened forged or rolled SAE 1050 (CK45) steel rolls; welded steel frames
  • Cone-bending device and overload protection standard
  • Remote technical support via Wi-Fi; built to EC safety directives (CE marked)

Questions we get asked about hydraulic 4-roll plate rollers

3-roll or 4-roll plate roller — which one do I need?

A 4-roll machine holds the sheet pinched between the top and bottom roll while the side rolls pre-bend and roll it, so both ends are pre-bent without turning the plate around and the operator never loses alignment — the better choice for production, thin or polished material and cones. A 3-roll initial-pinch machine does the same job with one more handling step at a lower price, which is why most job shops start with an MRM-H or MRM-S.

What does "capacity at 1.5× / 5× top roll Ø" mean?

Plate rolls are rated for a given diameter: the thicker the plate, the larger the minimum cylinder it can form. The manufacturer states two figures — the thickness the machine rolls into a tight cylinder of 1.5 times the top roll diameter, and the thickness it rolls at 5 times the top roll diameter. Tell us your plate thickness and the smallest diameter you make and we will size the machine from the capacity chart.

Can a 4R HMS roll cones?

Yes — a cone-bending device is standard on every 4R HMS, and the NC control stores the roll positions for repeat cones. Hydraulic side supports and the overhead crane keep large cones and thin material in shape while rolling.

How automatic can a plate roller be?

With the NC control the operator programs the roll positions once; the machine then bends to the stored program. Adding the vacuum sheet feeder, the hydraulic overhead crane and the part ejector turns the 4R HMS into a full automation line where the operator only loads blanks and removes finished cylinders at the welding station.

What voltage do İşitan plate rollers run on?

Three-phase machines are supplied for 600 V as standard with a CSA-approved panel; 208 V, 240 V and 480 V 3-phase are available on request, and 240 V single-phase on models with a small enough motor. Manual slip rolls have no electrics.
