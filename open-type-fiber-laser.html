Open-type fiber lasers — single-table and semi-closed, 5 × 10 to 85 × 10.5 ft, 1.5 to 60 kW

An open-type fiber laser has a single table that is loaded by hand, forklift or crane from any side and stops while the sheet is changed — the lowest-priced fiber laser, the first laser for many shops, and the only kind that cuts 20 to 85 ft plate on one table. NovoTech sells two lines in Canada, both on this page: the premium Dener Smart open-type, 3 or 6 kW in 5 × 10 to 6.6 × 20 ft, and the Smart semi-closed single-table machines from 8.2 × 20 to 13 × 82 ft for long plate, all made in Turkey; and the value line — NovoTech's own ULF open lasers from 10 × 5 to 85 × 10.5 ft and 1.5 to 60 kW, and the NP series from 5 × 10 to 6.6 × 20 ft to 12 kW. Every model is in the table below with its working area, power and speed, then each family with its full specification, photos and brochure. Every machine comes with a CSA-approved panel, chiller and nesting software, installed, trained and supported from Mississauga, ON, Canada. For a machine that cuts while you load, see the enclosed lasers; for heavy plate the plate lasers.

  • Premium: Smart open-type 3 – 6 kW, 5 × 10 to 6.6 × 20 ft; Smart semi-closed single tables 8.2 × 20 to 13 × 82 ft, 3 – 40 kW
  • Value: ULF open lasers 10 × 5 to 85 × 10.5 ft, 1.5 – 60 kW; NP series 5 × 10 to 6.6 × 20 ft, 3 – 12 kW
  • Loaded from any side; partial enclosures and fume extraction under the table; towers for the semi-closed machines

Questions we get asked about open-type fiber lasers

Open-type or enclosed — which should I buy?

Open-type when the budget comes first, when sheets are loaded by forklift or crane from the side, or when the plate is longer than any exchange table: the machine stops while you change the sheet, which is fine for a few sheets an hour. Enclosed when you cut all day: the exchange table or pallet changer keeps the laser cutting while you load, and the cabin keeps fumes and the beam inside. Many shops start with an open-type laser and add an enclosed one later.

Premium line or value line?

The premium Smart open and semi-closed lasers are built by Dener in Turkey with Precitec heads and IPG / nLIGHT sources and parts stocked in Canada. NovoTech's value-line ULF and the NP series are built in China with their own or Raycus sources, in more sizes — to 85 ft and 60 kW — at a lower price; NovoTech stocks the parts and does the service. Same cut quality at the same power; the premium machine lasts longer at high duty.

ULF-G or ULF-E?

Both are open single-table lasers. The G build takes 1.5 to 6 kW and moves at 295 ft/min with 1.4 G — plenty for sheet and light plate. The E build has a heavier frame for 1.5 to 12 kW, 459 ft/min and 1.7 G, a 7.2 ft wide table option (4022E, 6022E) and higher table loads; the large-format E machines from 20 × 8.5 to 85 × 10.5 ft take 6 to 60 kW for plate. Choose the E if you plan to go above 6 kW later or cut thick plate every day.

What laser power do I need?

Power sets the thickness and the speed. As a guide for mild steel: 3 kW cuts 1/2″, 6 kW 3/4″ (and 1/4″ at over 300 in/min), 12 kW 1″, 20 kW 1-1/4″ and 30 – 40 kW 1-1/2″ and beyond; stainless and aluminum run about 20 – 30 % thinner at the same power, with nitrogen or air assist. Most Canadian job shops buying today choose 6 or 12 kW: thick enough for structural work, fast enough to pay for the machine on thin sheet. Tell us your material mix and we run the cutting chart for you.

Which table size?

The model number is the nominal working area in centimetres: 3015 = 10 × 5 ft (3,000 × 1,500 mm) — a standard 4 × 8 or 5 × 10 sheet; 4020 = 13 × 6.6 ft; 6020 = 20 × 6.6 ft for 6 × 20 plate; 6025 and 8025 = 20 and 26 ft × 8.2 ft; 12526 = 41 × 8.5 ft. The 5 × 10 machine is the Canadian standard; buy the 6020 if you cut 20 ft plate or want to nest several sheets without reloading; the 4 × 4 ft Senfeng compact is for small parts in a small shop.

What about assist gas and fume extraction?

Oxygen gives the fastest cut and an oxide edge on mild steel; nitrogen a clean, paint-ready edge on all metals; compressed air at 16 bar replaces nitrogen on sheet to about 1/4″ and cuts the gas bill to near zero — the intelligent air-cutting package is an option on both lines. The machines have pneumatic extraction compartments under the table and connect to a dust collector, which we can supply.

What does the machine need on site?

A level floor, 600 V 3-phase (with the transformer we supply) or 480 V, a chiller (included), assist gas, a dust collector and a compressed-air supply for air cutting. A 6 kW 3015 draws about 30 kW in total and needs roughly 33 × 20 ft with a pallet changer. Delivery, installation and operator training are done by NovoTech from Mississauga, ON, Canada, and the Ontario pre-start health and safety review is prepared by us.

What voltage do these machines run on?

Three-phase machines are supplied for 600 V as standard with a CSA-approved panel; 208 V, 240 V and 480 V 3-phase are available on request, and 240 V single-phase on models whose main motor is 2.2 kW (3 HP) or less — a machine with a motor above 2.2 kW must run on three-phase power and cannot be converted to 240 V single-phase. Manual machines have no electrics.
