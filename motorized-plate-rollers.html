Motorized 3-roll plate rollers

Mechanical plate rolling machines for aluminum, HVAC ducting, signage panels, machine guards and sheet-metal enclosures: a planetary gearbox or gear train drives the rolls, the back roll is adjusted by motor or hand wheel and a manual drop-end releases the cylinder. The MRM-S rolls up to 3/8″ plate on working lengths to 10 ft and has a composite-panel version with a Delrin-coated top roll; the IRM and RM are the value-priced motorized plate rollers small workshops ask for. All with CSA-approved panels, parts and support from Mississauga, ON.

Questions we get asked about motorized 3-roll plate rollers

MRM-S, IRM or RM — what is the difference?

Roll diameter and construction. The MRM-S has 130–220 mm rolls on a welded frame with planetary drive and motorized back roll and takes plate to 3/8″; the IRM has 110–140 mm rolls on cast-iron frames for sheet to 3/16″; the RM has 90–95 mm rolls on a reinforced steel frame for sheet to 11 ga. Choose by the thickest sheet and the smallest diameter you roll.

Can I roll aluminum composite panels (Alucobond) without marking them?

Yes — the MRM-S composite-panel version has a top roll covered in segmented hard-plastic (Delrin) rings so the painted panel is not marked. It is used for curved signage, architectural cladding and outdoor design work.

Do motorized plate rollers bend cones?

Every MRM-S, IRM and RM has a cone-bending device: one end of the top roll is lowered so the sheet travels faster on the wide end. The optional digital readout on the back roll makes repeat cones easier.

What voltage do İşitan plate rollers run on?

Three-phase machines are supplied for 600 V as standard with a CSA-approved panel; 208 V, 240 V and 480 V 3-phase are available on request, and 240 V single-phase on models with a small enough motor. Manual slip rolls have no electrics.
