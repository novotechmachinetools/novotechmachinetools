Compact hydraulic press brakes — 16 to 66 US tons (15 to 60 t), 2 to 8 ft

A compact hydraulic press brake bends sheet between a punch and a V-die like its big brothers, at the size that fits a sheet-metal shop rather than a steel-fabrication plant: 2 to 8 ft of bending length and 16 to 66 US tons (15 to 60 t), on a footprint that goes through a door and runs on a 600 V or 208 V supply. NovoTech sells three on this page: the Dener XL 1560 — the smallest machine of the premium line, 66 US tons (60 t) over 5 ft with the 15.6″ ESA S840 2D control, Y1-Y2 ram on linear scales, a ball-screw X back gauge and EUROSTAMP tooling in the crate, built in Turkey; NovoTech's own value-line compacts, built to our specification — the WAD-50T CNC brakes, 55 US tons (50 t) over 4.3 and 5.2 ft with servo valves and a Delem DA-53Tx, and the WC67K-30T torsion-bar NC brake, 33 US tons (30 t) over 5.2 ft with an Estun E21PS; and the value-line HBV bending brakes, 16 to 35 tons (15 to 32 t), with a four-way V-block (HBV24 / 48) or two cylinders (HBV-1250 / 2540) for brackets, clips and short parts in plate to 3/16″. For HVAC fittings, enclosures, signage, brackets and job-shop work where a 100-ton (91 t) machine is more than you need. Every machine comes with a CSA-approved panel and a 1-year warranty, installed, trained and supported from Mississauga, ON, Canada. For 10 ft and longer, or 1/4″ plate and up, see the hydraulic press brakes; for a brake with no hydraulic oil the electric press brakes.

  • Dener XL 1560: 66 US tons (60 t) × 5 ft, 15.7″ throat, ESA S840 2D control, Y1-Y2-X axes on linear scales, EUROSTAMP 1281 punch and 2067 4-way die included — premium line, made in Turkey
  • Value line: WAD-50T/1300 and 1600 CNC brakes, 55 US tons (50 t) × 4.3 – 5.2 ft, 4 axes with Delem DA-53Tx; WC67K-30T/1600 NC torsion-bar brake, 33 US tons (30 t) × 5.2 ft with Estun E21PS
  • Value-line HBV bending brakes: 16.5 – 35 US tons (15 – 32 t), 5/32″ × 24 – 48″, 3/16″ × 49″ and 1/8″ × 100″, four-way V-block or two cylinders

Control — ESA S840 2D as standard; ESA S875 W, Delem DA-66S or DA-69S as options

Every XL comes with the 15.6″ ESA S840 touch screen: numeric and 2D graphic programming, automatic calculation of tonnage, bend sequence and back-gauge positions, tool library, up to 6 axes, USB and network. For shops that program from CAD or run many different parts, Dener fits the 21.5″ ESA S875 W (3D programming and simulation, DXF / STEP import, EsaBend 3D), the 24″ Delem DA-66S (2D graphic programming with a 3D machine view, Profile-S2D offline) or the 24″ Delem DA-69S (full 3D programming and simulation, Profile-S3D) — the same four controls on every Dener press brake. All four have collision control: once the part is drawn or imported, the control simulates the bend sequence and checks the part against the punch, the die and the machine before the first bend.

  • ESA S8402D programming
  • ESA S875 W3D programming

Questions we get asked about compact press brakes

What tonnage do I need?

Bending force depends on the sheet thickness, the bend length and the V-die opening: F = S² × 2 × R ÷ (1.4 × V) tonnes per metre with S and V in millimetres and R = 47 kg/mm² for mild steel — in inches and US tons per foot about 575 × t² ÷ V. In a V of 8 × the thickness, a 4 ft bend takes about 16 US tons (15 t) in 16 ga, 39 tons (35 t) in 10 ga and 47 tons (43 t) in 8 ga (0.16″); a wider die lowers the force — 8 ga in a 2″ V takes about 31 tons (28 t) over 4 ft — at the cost of a larger inside radius. The control calculates the exact force for the tool and material you enter; send us your thickest and longest bend and we size the machine.

Dener XL 1560 or a value-line compact?

The XL 1560 is the premium machine: the heaviest frame, servo-valve hydraulics, an ESA 2D control with collision control, the DSP-AP laser finger guard as standard, every Dener option open to it — 3D controls, 4- to 6-axis back gauges, CNC crowning, Wila clamping, robot cells — and parts stocked in Canada; it bends 3/16″ over the full 5 ft and 1/4″ over 4 ft. The value-line WAD-50T is the same electro-hydraulic servo architecture (servo valves, linear scales, Delem control) built to our specification at a lower price, for 1/8″ over 5.2 ft; the WC67K-30T is the simple torsion-bar NC brake for one material and repeat parts. If the brake runs all day or you program from CAD, the XL; if it bends brackets and enclosures a few hours a day, the value line.

NC or CNC?

An NC brake (the WC67K-30T) sets the ram depth and the back gauge from a stored program, with the two cylinders kept in step by a torsion bar — enough for the same parts in one material. A CNC brake (the XL 1560 and the WAD-50T) measures the ram on linear scales and corrects it through servo valves, calculates tonnage, bend sequence and gauge positions from the drawing, and changes material and tool in the program — the machine for mixed work, tight angles and multi-bend parts. The buying guide on the press brake page compares every control these machines are sold with.

Press brake or HBV bending brake?

The XL 1560, WAD-50T and WC67K-30T are press brakes: a programmable ram, a motorised back gauge, segmented tooling and a guard, for sheet work all day. The HBV is a hydraulic bending brake for short parts in plate — brackets, clips, tabs — bent on a four-way V-block (HBV24 / 48) or between two cylinders (HBV-1250 / 2540) with a hand-wheel back gauge, at a fraction of the price. If you bend panels and enclosures, a press brake; if you bend 3/16″ plate brackets a few at a time, the HBV.

What tooling fits, and do you stock it?

European-style (Promecam / Amada) segmented tooling fits every line: the premium Dener brakes carry European-style top-tool holders, the value-line brakes their own clamping, and a sectioned punch set with a 2-V or 4-V die ships with every machine (a 4-V die with the Dener brakes, a 2-V die with the value line) — tell us which tooling standard your shop already has and we fit the holders to match. NovoTech stocks Eurostamp press brake tooling in Canada — punches, dies, radius, hemming and joggle tools, gooseneck and special profiles — and sizes it to your machine; quick-release, pneumatic and Wila hydraulic clamping and American-style adapters are options, not standard equipment.

Are they safe to run without a separate guarding system?

Every compact press brake carries side guards, an enclosed rear guard with interlocked doors and a foot pedal with emergency stop; the Dener XL 1560 comes with the DSP-AP laser finger guard and an MCS safety PLC as standard, and on the value-line machines a laser guard — DSP, Fiessler AKAS or a light curtain — is an option we recommend. In Ontario a new press brake needs a pre-start health and safety review (PSR) under Regulation 851 before it runs.

In every case unloading is the buyer's responsibility and expense — the crane or forklift, the rigging and moving the machine onto its foundation — so book a rigger for the delivery day; we are glad to recommend riggers who know press brakes and to tell you the weight and lifting points.

Is the hydraulic oil included?

No — the hydraulic oil is bought by the customer. Before commissioning, fill the tank with ISO VG 46 anti-wear hydraulic oil (AW 46) in the quantity shown in the specification table for your model; our technician checks the level and bleeds the system during installation. Electric press brakes have no hydraulic oil at all.

Manual machines have no electrics.
